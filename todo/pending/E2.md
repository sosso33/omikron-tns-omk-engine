## 6. For the channel's owner - `engine/src/actor/channel.cpp`

Two lines of `Cef_TickChannel`'s commit are missing from `CefChannel::tick`
(the block after `if (word != lastInput_)`), and one convention differs:

1. `LABEL_75` (29_win32.c 306-312): `if (n == 1 && (queue[0] &
   0x40000000)) { queue[0] = 0; n = 0; }` - a lone idle word is dropped
   before the push. Without it a pressed word queues BEHIND the idle word
   `setBankGroup`/`resetQueue` seed and the machine stalls until something
   pops it.
2. Under the CURRENT state's flag 0x20000000 with `n > 1`, the queue is cut
   to 1 (`count = 1`) instead of pushed.
3. `tick(dt, 0)` substitutes the queue front for a zero code. The engine's
   polled word is never 0 - `sub_4A7A20` makes it 0x40000000 when nothing is
   held - so a caller driving from a keyboard must pass `kIdleInput`; the
   controller does. The AI path is unaffected (it injects).

`PlayerController` shadows (1) - a reset counted in `stats().queueResets`, or
a pressed word that `findTransition` says opens an edge while no transition
happened, triggers `injectInput({word})` - and can drop the shadow when (1)
lands. `verify.py: engine actor states` cannot see either, since its sweep
injects.
